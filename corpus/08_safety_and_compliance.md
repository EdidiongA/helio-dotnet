# Safety and compliance

Installations should follow IEC 62548 for PV array design and IEC 60364-7-712 for the electrical installation of PV systems. In Nigeria, the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission's mini-grid and captive power rules apply to systems above 1 MW; residential systems below that threshold need no generation licence but must still meet the wiring regulations.

Lithium batteries must be installed in a ventilated space away from living areas, on a non-combustible surface, with at least 500 mm clearance from the inverter and any heat source. Never install a lithium bank in a sealed cupboard or a loft; summer loft temperatures in Lagos exceed 50 degrees Celsius and shorten battery life.

Keep a fire extinguisher rated for electrical fires near the inverter. Water must never be used on a lithium battery fire; a Class D extinguisher or dry sand is the correct first response, and the area should be evacuated because the smoke is toxic.

Label the DC isolator, AC isolator and battery breaker clearly, and leave a single-line diagram of the installation in a sleeve next to the inverter. Emergency responders and future technicians need to know how to isolate the system.

Rooftop work requires fall protection above 2 m. Panels should be fixed to rails anchored into roof structure, not into sheet metal alone; a 2 m by 1 m panel presents about 240 kg of uplift force in a 120 km/h gust.
